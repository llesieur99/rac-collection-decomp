# Credits

## The project

* The contributor of this repository, who owns the release it studies and
  wrote everything in `src/`, `tools/`, `tests/`, `config/` and `docs/`.

## The method and the vocabulary

The evidence standard, the source layout, the gates and the naming rules come
from the sibling Ratchet & Clank decompilation projects, all authored by the
same contributor and all under GPL-3.0:

* `rac1-decomp` — Ratchet & Clank (2002)
* `rac2-gc-decomp` — Ratchet & Clank: Going Commando (PS2)
* `rac3-uya-decomp` — Ratchet & Clank: Up Your Arsenal (PS2)
* `rac5-sm-decomp` — Ratchet & Clank: Size Matters (PS2)
* `rac-sac-decomp` — Ratchet & Clank: Secret Agent Clank (PS2)
* `rac8-act-decomp` — Ratchet & Clank: A Crack in Time (PS3)

`tools/ppu_elf.py` is shared with `rac8-act-decomp`.

## The formats

The SELF container and the NPDRM decryption scheme are implemented by RPCS3,
under its own licence. No RPCS3 code is linked here; the scheme is documented
and the reader is pointed at the RPCS3 sources.

## The game

Ratchet & Clank Collection was developed by Insomniac Games and published by
Sony Interactive Entertainment. This project is an unofficial fan project, not
affiliated with or endorsed by either.
